Eileen M. Smith was a watercolorist who spent decades recording the streets and houses of London. Her signed and dated works run from the 1960s to the late 1990s and take in Hampstead, Charles Street and Shepherd Market in Mayfair, Upper Harley Street near Regent's Park, and Whitehall. She worked in pen and watercolor, carefully drawing each doorway, shop sign and window box, and she usually wrote the address and the year right on the sheet. That habit makes her pictures small historical records as much as paintings. Her work turns up regularly at British and Irish salerooms, and Shepherd Market, the tiny village of lanes behind Piccadilly, was clearly a favorite: another view of it sold at auction in 2023. Little else about her life has been published, and we have not been able to confirm her dates or training. Our 1975 Shepherd Market, with its antique and curio shops, shows her at her most detailed.
