*Old Stage Gate* is signed "E Dowling 1953," and the typed exhibition tag on its back gives the artist's name as Eugene Dowling. We attribute it to Eugene H. Dowling (1879–1961) of Portland, Oregon. The attribution rests on circumstantial evidence: the name matches, the painting surfaced in the Portland area, and the Dowling household was an artistic one.

Born in Iowa in 1879, Eugene H. Dowling worked in Portland as a machinist and boilermaker in the 1900s and later as an attorney. He lived on SE Belmont Street with his wife, Colista Murray Dowling (1881–1968), an established Portland painter and illustrator and a teacher and officer of the Oregon Society of Artists. He was in his seventies in 1953, when the painting was made. He died in Portland on May 14, 1961.
