F. Byers is one of the many painters whose names survive mainly on the backs of their pictures. *The Wharf* still carries its original label from El Gatito Gallery in Los Gatos, California, an artists' cooperative founded around 1960 that thrived for more than two decades until the Loma Prieta earthquake destroyed it in 1989. Its members were working painters of the Santa Clara Valley, among them San Jose State graduates, watercolor society regulars, and harbor painters who worked with the palette knife. This oil fits that world well: a harbor and its sailboats pared down to bold sweeps of red, white and yellow over pale blue water. Its modest gallery price tag suggests it was painted and sold in the mid-century years, though we have not been able to date it, and we have found no other record of the artist yet.
