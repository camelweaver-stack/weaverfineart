Scott Golem is an American artist and designer whose prints grow out of four decades of graphic design. Raised in the industrial Detroit area, he holds a BA from Michigan State University and an MFA from the University of North Texas. His design career took him from packaging and merchandising for Dr Pepper and JCPenney to children's books for Pearson Education and art direction for technology magazines, and he has spent eighteen years teaching design at the university level, most recently as a professor at Eastern New Mexico University in Portales. In his own art he calls the approach "digitally abstracted, photographically-based collage," built from the textures he loves: weathered architecture, worn surfaces, machinery and type. His 2019 exhibition *Circling the Horizon* at ENMU's Runnels Gallery turned the big skies around Portales into rings of color. In 2023 he was an artist in residence at Willow House in Terlingua, Texas, near Big Bend. *Arrangement 14b* (2016), from his hybrid digital monoprint work, is signed, titled and dated by the artist.
