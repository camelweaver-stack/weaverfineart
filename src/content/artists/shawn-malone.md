Shawn Malone is a landscape photographer who has made Marquette, Michigan, and the shores of Lake Superior her life's subject. She came to the Upper Peninsula in 2000, drawn by the lake's clear blue-green water, and turned to photography full time in 2006. For years she showed at juried art fairs around the country, including the Ann Arbor Art Fair, before opening Lake Superior Photo, her gallery and studio on South Front Street in downtown Marquette. She is best known for night skies: her aurora time-lapse *North Country Dreamland* won People's Choice in a 2013 Smithsonian online competition, and her northern lights footage has appeared in National Geographic's "America's National Parks." Her style favors honest, lightly processed images, printed by her own hand on archival materials. Her husband, Brian Malone, is also a photographer. These two signed views of Marquette's Lower Harbor, one at sunrise over the ore dock and one at night, come straight from the waterfront she has photographed for more than two decades.
