Eugene H. Dowling was born in Iowa in 1879 and spent his early childhood in Colorado. By 1900 he was living in Portland, Oregon, where he worked as a machinist and boilermaker before becoming an attorney. He practiced law in Portland from the 1910s through at least 1950. He died in Portland in 1961.

He married Colista Murray, known professionally as Colista Dowling (1881–1968). She was a Portland painter and illustrator for about sixty years, trained at the Art Students League, the Pennsylvania Academy of the Fine Arts and the Drexel Institute, and later in Oregon. She illustrated for *The Pacific Monthly*, painted murals for clubs, hotels and churches, exhibited at the Portland Art Museum and the Seattle Art Museum, and served as an officer of the Oregon Society of Artists in 1929 and 1940. The society's history credits her among the instructors whose classes, exhibitions and bazaars helped fund its building; a Colista Dowling memorial gallery was established there in 1970. Her work is held by the Oregon Historical Society. These facts are about Colista; no exhibition or painter's listing for Eugene has yet been found.

The gallery attributes *Old Stage Gate, Ramona's Marriage Place*, an oil signed "E Dowling" and dated 1953, to him. The painting came from Oregon, and its back carries his name, "Eugene H. Dowling," in pencil, along with a typed entry tag from a 1953 show. The exhibition has not yet been identified, and the gallery is still researching his work as a painter.
