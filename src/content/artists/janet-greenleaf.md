Janet Edna Greenleaf also painted and exhibited as Janet E. Judge, her married name. According to a biography published by the Greenleaf Gallery, which askART reproduces from information supplied by the artist's granddaughter, she was born in Peru, Indiana, grew up in the San Francisco Bay Area and, at the age of ten, was enrolled in the Saturday art school for children at the California College of Arts and Crafts. She earned a BA in art, in design and art history, at the University of California, Berkeley, taught first and second grade in Oakland for three years, and continued her art studies with John Gill.

She married in 1953. By her own account she held 24 one-person shows in galleries in San Francisco, Piedmont, Oakland, Burlingame, Fresno and San Jose, helped other artists market their work, and opened her own gallery in 1979. For much of her life she lived near Smithfield, Rhode Island, and exhibited in Rhode Island and eastern Connecticut. She died in 2003.

She is best known for small oil still lifes of flowers in vases, which dealers describe as frequently found in the Northeast, and in later years she also painted landscapes in watercolor. Sources disagree on her birth year: auction houses give 1936, while one dealer gives 1916 and notes that some give 1936. The gallery has not settled it and does not print one.

*Children in a Flower Field* is signed "J. Greenleaf" and is attributed to her on that signature, the Bay Area origin of the painting, and the seller's identification. It is much larger than the still lifes she is known for, and the gallery has not found another children's scene by her. The same signature was also used by Jacob I. Greenleaf (1887–1968) of Rockport, Massachusetts, a landscape painter, whose subjects do not resemble this picture.
