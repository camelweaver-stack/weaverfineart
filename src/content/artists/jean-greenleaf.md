Very little is known about Jean Greenleaf. The gallery knows her only from paintings signed "J. Greenleaf," and her full name comes from a single piece of evidence: a pencil inscription on the stretcher bar of a landscape titled *Blue Lake*, signed "J. Greenleaf" and dated 1962, which reads "Jean Greenleaf" with an address in Campbell, California. That painting, a 28 by 22 inch oil of a lake in a wooded meadow, was offered in 2026 by a San Jose dealer. It is the only dated work the gallery has found, so her working years are given here only as the 1960s.

*Children in a Flower Field* carries the same signature and comes from the same part of California: it was sold in 2026 by Goodwill of Silicon Valley in San Jose, which offered a second signed "J Greenleaf" oil of a girl in a meadow overlooking sailboats at the same time. The three works connect by name and place, not by a record of the artist, and the inscription could be an owner's as easily as the painter's. The gallery has found no biography, exhibition record, obituary or auction result for a Jean Greenleaf of Campbell, and no birth or death dates, so none are given here. Other painters named Greenleaf are recorded elsewhere in the country and appear to be different artists. This page will be updated if more is found.
