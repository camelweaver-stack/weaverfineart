A 1953 oil of the old stage gate at "Ramona's Marriage Place," the Casa de Estudillo in Old Town San Diego, which was run as a tourist attraction from 1910 into the 1960s. A weathered plank gate stands under a heavy timber lintel in the adobe wall, shaded by a pepper tree, with barrels beside it and a sunlit dirt courtyard in front.

The title and subject are written on the back in pencil, and the painting still carries its original typed entry tag from an art society or club show, where it was offered at $50.
