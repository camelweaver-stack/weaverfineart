Eugene H. Dowling was born in Iowa in 1879 and spent his early childhood in Colorado. By 1900 he was living in Portland, Oregon, where he worked as a machinist and boilermaker before becoming an attorney. He practiced law in Portland from the 1910s through at least 1950. He married Colista Murray, who became one of Portland's best-known painters and illustrators and an officer and teacher of the Oregon Society of Artists. The couple lived for decades on Belmont Street in southeast Portland. He died in Portland in 1961.

The gallery attributes *Old Stage Gate, Ramona's Marriage Place*, an oil signed "E Dowling" and dated 1953, to him. The painting came from Oregon, and its back carries his name, "Eugene H. Dowling," in pencil, along with a typed entry tag from a 1953 show. The exhibition has not yet been identified, and the gallery is still researching his work as a painter.
