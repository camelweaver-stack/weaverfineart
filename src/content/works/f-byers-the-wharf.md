A mid-century abstract view of a harbor. Sailboats sit at a wharf under broad strokes of red, white, and yellow, with buildings at the left and right edges and pale blue water in front.

The original label from El Gatito Gallery is still on the back. El Gatito was an artists' gallery in Los Gatos, California, founded about 1960. It ran for more than twenty years until the 1989 Loma Prieta earthquake destroyed it. Other artists who showed there included Dennise Meier, Jack Wilson, Elizabeth Bourdet, and Gisela Embree.
