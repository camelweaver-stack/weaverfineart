Massimiliano "Max" Balletti paints the streets of Rome, and for years he has done it in the open air, among the artists of Piazza Navona. Working with his brother as the Balletti Brothers, he draws the city in pen or pencil and finishes each sheet in watercolor by hand, with loose, lively lines and warm washes of color. Visitors who find their stall often mention the same thing: in a piazza where printed "originals" are common, the Ballettis sell work they made themselves, one sheet at a time. His paintings of Piazza Navona appeared alongside a 2011 article on the square's painters. Our sheet is a classic Roman street corner, with its tabacchi, stationery shop and ristorante signs, and his own "Made in Rome" label on the back. It is a genuine handmade memento of the Eternal City.
